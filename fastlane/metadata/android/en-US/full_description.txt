GHOST is a cyberpunk-themed, on-device embodied AI companion powered by Google LiteRT-LM and local Gemma weights.

Built for complete privacy and hardware integration, GHOST runs entirely on your phone with zero mandatory cloud telemetry.

Key Capabilities:
• Real-time local inference using LiteRT-LM (Gemma 4 E2B / E4B)
• Hardware telemetry integration (battery, thermals, sensors, system state)
• Diegetic voice interactions, kinetic reflexes & passive auditory cues
• Extensible local SKILL.md plugin system in Documents/GHOST/skills
• On-device multimodal vision and voice processing
• Cyberpunk-styled terminal interface and responsive edge visualizer
• 100% Free and Open Source (FOSS) with zero proprietary tracker SDKs.
